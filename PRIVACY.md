# Privacy Policy

**Inventory Buddy**

Effective as of 13 October 2026.

Inventory Buddy is a free, open-source Android app for tracking a restaurant's or kitchen's stock. It is published by Aaron ("**we**", "**us**" or "**our**"). This Privacy Policy describes how personal information is handled when you use the Inventory Buddy app (the "**Service**").

**In short:** Inventory Buddy has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. Everything you enter stays on your device. We never receive it. The only exceptions are the Google services described under "Third-party services" below, which you can choose not to use, and anything you choose to send yourself (for example, an order you share with a supplier).

## Personal information we collect

**We do not collect personal information through the app.** The app does not ask you to create an account or sign in. It does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools. Nothing you enter in the app is sent to us.

**Information you provide to us directly.** If you contact us (see "How to contact us" below), we receive the information you choose to include, such as your GitHub username and the contents of your message.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores the information you enter in a database on your device, so that it can show your stock, orders and reports. This includes:

- **Inventory records:** item names, units, categories, costs, reorder levels, barcodes and notes.
- **Stock history:** deliveries, usage, waste and count corrections, with their dates, amounts, costs and any notes you add.
- **Supplier details:** supplier names, phone numbers, email addresses and notes. These may be personal information about your suppliers or their staff. You are responsible for having their permission to store it.
- **Settings:** your chosen currency.

This information is not sent to us. We cannot see it, access it, change it or delete it.

## Third-party services

Some features use services provided by Google or by other apps on your device. These are governed by their own terms and privacy policies, not this one.

- **Barcode scanning (Google Play services).** When you tap a scan button, the app opens Google's code scanner, which is part of Google Play services. The camera and scanning are handled by Google Play services, and only the scanned barcode text is returned to the app. The app itself has no camera permission. According to Google, the ML Kit libraries used for scanning may contact Google's servers to receive updates and may send information about how the scanner performs and is used. See Google's ML Kit documentation (https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). If you do not want to use this service, do not use the scan buttons. Every other feature works without them.
- **Android backup (Google).** If you have turned on backup in your device settings, Android may include the app's data in your device backup, which is stored in your own Google account under Google's terms. You control this in your device's backup settings.
- **Sending orders and calling suppliers.** When you tap **Send** on an order, Android opens its share menu and you choose an app (for example email, SMS or WhatsApp). The order text, and the supplier's email address if you saved one, are passed to the app you choose. When you tap **Call**, your phone app opens with the supplier's number filled in. What happens next is governed by those apps.

## How we use your personal information

We do not receive personal information from the app, so we do not use it.

If you contact us, we use the information you provide only to respond to you, to fix problems and to improve the Service.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or training AI models.

## How we share your personal information

We do not share personal information from the app, because we do not receive it.

If you contact us through GitHub, your message is handled by GitHub under its own privacy statement (https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). GitHub Issues are public. Please do not post personal or business information there.

We may disclose information you send us if required by law, or to protect our rights or the rights of others.

## Retention

Information in the app stays on your device until you delete it. Removed items are archived so their history stays available in reports. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes everything stored by the app on that device. It does not delete copies in your device backup, or messages you have already sent through other apps.

We keep messages you send us for as long as needed to respond and to keep a record of the issue.

## Your choices

- **View, correct or delete your information** directly in the app. You can edit or remove items and suppliers, and delete individual history entries.
- **Delete everything** by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your device settings.
- **Barcode scanning is optional.** You can type barcodes and search by name instead.
- **Backup:** turn device backup on or off in your device settings.

## Security

Your information is stored on your device and protected by your device's own security. Anyone who can use your unlocked device can open the app and see its contents. We recommend using a screen lock. No method of storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information on your device.

## International data transfer

We do not transfer personal information, because we do not receive it from the app. Google and GitHub may process information in other countries under their own policies.

## Children

The Service is a business tool and is not intended for use by anyone under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example if the app gains features that handle information differently. If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this policy and publish the new version in the project repository (https://github.com/jammy-007/Inventory-Buddy/blob/main/PRIVACY.md) and in the release notes. The history of every change is visible in the repository.

## How to contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our practices, please open an issue at https://github.com/jammy-007/Inventory-Buddy/issues. Issues are public, so please do not include personal information in them.

## Notice to European users

**Information you send us.** We are the controller of personal data you send us when you contact us. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to you and maintaining the Service.

**Your rights.** You may ask us to access, correct, delete, restrict or transfer personal data you have sent us, and you may object to our processing of it. You may also complain to the data protection regulator where you live. For the UK, this is the Information Commissioner's Office (https://ico.org.uk).
